KNOW THE TACTICS

Scams come in many forms.

Recognizing the approach makes it easier to stop and verify.

03 / LEARN
☎

Fraudulent calls

Someone impersonates a bank, government agency, or support team and pressures you to act. They may ask for an OTP, PIN, or remote access.

Red flag → urgency, threats, or requests for secrets
✉

SMS & WhatsApp

Unexpected links, delivery alerts, job offers, or urgent requests arrive in a message and push you to respond quickly.

Red flag → urgency + unfamiliar link
⌘

Fake websites

Lookalike pages imitate trusted brands to capture your login or payment details. A padlock alone does not prove a site is genuine.

Red flag → strange domain or spelling
▦

QR-code scams

A QR code can send you to a fake payment or sign-in page. Check the destination and payment details before proceeding.

Red flag → unexpected payment request
♙

Phishing

Messages pretend to be from a known organization to trick you into clicking, downloading, or sharing personal data.

Red flag → asks you to “verify” account details
₹

Payment & refund scams

Scammers may pose as buyers, sellers, or support staff. A request to enter a UPI PIN is for authorizing a payment, not receiving one.

Red flag → asks you to pay to receive money
