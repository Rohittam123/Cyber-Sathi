PAUSE BEFORE YOU ACT

Your practical safety guide.

Use these steps when a call, message, link, or payment request feels unexpected.

04 / TAKE ACTION
01

Pause and resist pressure

Urgency, threats, secrecy, or promises of a reward are reasons to slow down. Do not click, reply, transfer money, or install software while pressured.

02

Verify through a separate channel

Open the official app or type a known website address yourself. Find the organization’s contact details independently instead of using the message’s link or phone number.

03

Protect your private details

Never share an OTP, password, PIN, CVV, or UPI PIN with a caller or message sender. Do not grant screen sharing or remote access to someone who contacted you unexpectedly.

04

If you clicked or shared information

Contact your bank or payment provider using official details. Change affected passwords from a trusted device, enable two-step verification, and preserve relevant messages and transaction records.

05

Report suspected fraud

In India, report cyber financial fraud promptly by calling 1930 and visit cybercrime.gov.in. If you are elsewhere, use your local cybercrime reporting authority.
