ABOUT CYBERSAATHI

Awareness is a first step to safer choices.

CyberSaathi is an educational assistant for building confidence around suspicious calls, messages, websites, and payment requests.

Understand the message checker

The checker uses browser-based rules to flag common patterns. It does not upload message text, and it cannot confirm that a message is safe.

◇

Your privacy and progress

Checklist and quiz progress is stored locally in this browser. Scam-check reports remain in your browser while being analyzed. If you explicitly save a detailed report, its original message, risk assessment, warning signs, and recommended actions are stored in your signed-in Firebase account and visible only to that account.

Please remember: CyberSaathi is an awareness tool, not a security verdict, cybersecurity service, or law-enforcement service. For urgent financial fraud in India, contact your bank and call 1930.
